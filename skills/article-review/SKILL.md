# Article Review

Review the supplied text at the requested depth. Preserve the author's intent and voice, distinguish taste from concrete problems, and prioritize edits that improve reader value.

## Choose the Review Scope

- A general article review uses the full editorial report below.
- A focused critique, quick review, or requested passage rewrite follows that narrower scope; do not force scores or a full report when they do not serve the request.
- The text is required. Audience, title, and publication context are optional unless their absence materially changes the review. State reasonable assumptions rather than blocking on them.

## Full Editorial Report

Choose the dominant lens: **A, information and utility** for technical or argument-driven writing; **B, emotion and empathy** for essays and voice-driven writing. Note a secondary lens only if it matters.

Use [review-rubric.md](references/review-rubric.md) for the seven dimensions and scoring, and [report-template.md](references/report-template.md) for the full report shape. These references are for a full or scored review, not prerequisites for every line edit.

Score each dimension from 0.0 to 5.0 with one decimal place. The overall score is editorial judgment rather than an arithmetic mean. Higher safety scores mean safer; below 3.0, identify the concrete publication risk and needed revision. Support scores with textual evidence rather than confidence in an author's intent.

Include the mode, overall/safety/human-distinctiveness scores, dimension findings, up to three prioritized improvements, and one to three useful rewrite examples. Adapt the format to the user's requested depth.

## Evidence and Completion

Verify material factual claims against primary sources when needed and permitted. Label unverified claims and distinguish missing evidence from a disproved statement. Do not invent sources or experiences for a rewrite.

Finish when the requested review or rewrite is delivered with actionable findings and any material evidence gaps. Evaluate the text, not the author; do not add unrelated publishing or rewriting work.
