# Cognitive Rhythm in Writing

Help the reader follow changing observations, uncertainty, and conclusions. Rhythm should come from the subject and the writer's supported judgments, not commentary about what the document will do next.

- For openings, sentence rhythm, paragraph density, and endings, use [techniques.md](references/techniques.md).
- For diagnosing flat prose or distinguishing useful pauses from filler, use [diagnosis.md](references/diagnosis.md).
- Use `japanese-tech-writing` only when manuscript formatting or argument structure also needs attention.

Preserve necessary context and the author's voice. Treat suggested patterns as options, not quotas: do not force alternating sentence lengths, hesitation, or an unresolved question into every passage. If the source provides no material for a rhetorical turn, leave it plain.

Revise the requested passage and resolve the pacing problem without inventing facts, experiences, or artificial suspense. Keep the skill's technique names out of the finished prose unless those techniques are themselves the subject. Report remaining source gaps only when they affect the result.
